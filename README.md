# MY FAVORITE SERIES/MOVIE
## **Kimetsu No Yaiba**
> Kimetsu no Yaiba, also known as Demon Slayer, is a Japanese anime about Tanjiro Kamado, a kind-hearted boy who becomes a demon slayer after his family is killed by demons and his younger sister Nezuko is turned into one. The story follows Tanjiro on his journey to find a cure for his sister while fighting powerful demons and learning the importance of courage, family, and perseverance.
